HAPPIEST 18TH BIRTHDAYYYY!! 🎂♡ Welcome to chapter eighteen: more freedom, new experiences, big dreams, and a completely unhelpful amount of adulting paperwork. Please read the instructions carefully. (There are no instructions. We're all improvising.)

I hope you know how special you are—not just because it's your birthday, but because of the person you are on all the ordinary days too. I hope you get to laugh until your stomach hurts, make memories you keep forever, and feel loved without having to earn it.

And about NEET and your future: I know preparation can feel heavy sometimes. There may be days when the chapters seem endless, the questions are confusing, or you wonder if you're doing enough. Please be gentle with yourself. Take things one topic, one question, one day at a time. A tough mock test is feedback, not a prophecy. A break is not a betrayal of your dream.

I truly hope you get closer to the future you're working for. And whatever twists life brings, please remember that you are a whole person before you're ever a score, a rank, a college seat, or a job title. You deserve support, rest, friendship and happiness along the way—not only at the finish line.

So here's my birthday wish for you: courage when things are hard, peace when your mind is loud, people who make you feel safe, and lots of little moments that remind you life is bigger than exams. Keep dreaming, keep trying, and please don't forget to eat and sleep properly, future legend. 😤💗

Happy 18th, once again. I'm cheering for you—not only for what you achieve, but for you, the human being doing your best.
